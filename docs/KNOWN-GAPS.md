Here is the list of the elements that are already known to be missing or placeholders, if you have information about their original behavior, don't hesitate to post it, also, if you find something missing that is not part of that list, please report it so that i can investigate:
*Guesses are marked PLACEHOLDER in the code and listed in docs/PLACEHOLDERS.md. Any video or memory that settles one is welcome!*

**🗺️ Campaign: Howling Plains (AZ1), in story order**
• **Castle Crayburn**: the watchtower training battle is missing (not in the game files)
• **Hometowns**: the quest giver appears after you win the sparring match (guess); the dwarf elder's spot is a guess (no dwarf run on video)
• **Gaal fortunes**: which fortune you get is random (it looks random in the videos too)
• **Sea Hag**: the "Princess Revert" opening is never used (no run shows it)
• **Tranquil Dream**: Sister Midnight's Cunning / Instinct forms: where they start is guessed (never seen)
• **Fort Romor**: whether a defence you chose stays after a *lost* Lord battle is a guess (it's used up after a win)
• **The Usurper**: Shepherd's Rest (Finanif) reward is a guess (nobody on video fought it)
• **Sapphire Gate**: opens once you accept The Blood Beneath. Inferred, no video shows where the key comes from
• **Quests**: rewards never seen on video are guessed gold
• **All dungeons**: the "Challenge Completed" bonus gold (+100%) is missing (its condition was never found); Pappy Jasper's Whip (Dec 2016 drop) is missing

**🌊 Campaign: Alachian Sea (AZ2), in story order**
• **Lurking Crocodile**: the race card for Coyotle, Orc, Dwarf, Shin'hare and Vennen champions is inferred (seen for Necrotic, Elf and Human only)
• **AZ2 map battles**: same rewards for both factions (only the Underworld run was recorded)
• **Scylla & Charybdis**: 1-in-3 chance per voyage that they wake (the real trigger was on the server)
• **The Kraken**: Mayor Galway never dies (that ending was never seen); the sea hag curses and tentacles aren't reproduced
• **Ruins of Kukatan**: Tomb of Omens reward is a guess (never visited on video)
• **Great Machine Graveyard**: your Brutecrown ally if you skipped Brutecrown Bluff is a guess (Vargar); who plays first after the Roundabout's blast is a guess

**🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Mercenaries**
• Every upgrade costs the same (2 challenges + 25,000 gold): only one level's price is known
• Which dungeon room is the "mercenary challenge" room: random (the real rule is unknown)

**❄️ Frost Ring Arena**
• Missing: the Swarm! Swarm!, Rage and Charge! challenges (they change every card, and no game card does that)
• Guessed: one challenge per tier; which bosses in which tier; how often Uruunaz and Zakiir show up; the top tiers' decks; opponents' equipment; how gold is split between fights; what chests hold; who plays the new Runic / Banners challenges

**💰 Loot & economy** (most important first)
• Odds inside campaign packs, chests, the Wheel of Fate (reel colors) and boosters (sleeves, gift boxes)
• Chest rarity from boosters: uncommon 27%, rare 10%, legendary 4%, from about 150 packs opened on video (small sample)
• How many items legendary / primal chests hold
• PvE packs' exact contents; special packs' contents (race packs, Kismet's Draft, GenCon 2016)
• Store gold prices (boosters, special packs, stardust, Kismet packs, full sets)
• Account level rewards: the "fixed commons", which stardust and how much, which alternate art and at which level, which sleeve
• Holiday and sleeve season dates are guessed
