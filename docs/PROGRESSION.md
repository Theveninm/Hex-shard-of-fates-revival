# Progression, rewards and store

The original game decided rewards and store prices on its servers; the client files only hold
the champion XP table and the starter decks. HEX Revival therefore rebuilds progression from period
sources. This page lists every value with its source, so that **placeholders** (values chosen for
the mod, without a source) can be replaced when better information turns up.

All of it applies to accounts created without "Unlock everything" (those already own every card,
so card rewards are skipped; gold, XP and levels still count).

Code: `mods/HexRevival/src/HexRevival/Progression.cs` (rewards, levels), `Loot.cs` (campaign
packs, data generated by `tools/gen_loot.py`), `Store.cs`, `Collection.cs` (starter collection),
`Inventory.cs` (equipment earned). Each value is tagged there with `Src.*`.

Taming quest ("Tamed"): when a campaign battle is won and an opponent troop marked as tamed by the
taming item ended in the void, the creature's card is given (1 copy, once per champion and creature;
Game: the Tamed marker, HexServer: the capture rule).

Quests (Game: the quest templates, their objectives and the numbered conversations of each quest
giver). A node's dialogue follows the champion's progress in the quest (e.g. "Robot - Step 3 -
Install Ruby Mainline" once the ruby mainline is found). Mechanics and their sources:

- Gates: the Brink Ridge blockade (fight, sneak, or bribe for 1,000 gold: Game, the guard's answer),
  the Blood Gate (open once "The Blood Beneath" is started: the Blood Key), the Masked Army at the
  Vale of Oberon (open once Usurper / Fort Romor is started).
- Sapphire Gate: where the Sapphire Key came from is unknown, the gate is open (PLACEHOLDER).
- Gaal fortunes: 100 gold (Game, the dialogue); the fortune is a random "Fortune of ..." card of the
  game, applied at the start of the next campaign battle only (PLACEHOLDER: which fortune and how
  long it lasted are unknown).
- Gnomes Come Home: the gnomes found in the Lost Gnomes camp follow the champion; the Deep Desert
  battle happens only with them, and Herbert's camp completes the escort.
- Not playable yet: objectives in dungeons (Tranquil Dream, Smoldering Dead, Devonshire Keep,
  Usurper, Fort Romor), the boat to AZ2, the zombie invasion variants of some nodes.

The dev command `sim` plays a quest script on a throwaway champion (e.g. `sim new Human; visit
Node046; talk; visit Node047; talk; fight win; ...`) and writes the dialogues, quest steps and
rewards to HexRevivalSim.txt, without touching the account in use.

The loot tables are kept as published (`data/az1_pack_loot_tables.csv`); their misspelt names are
corrected in `data/loot_name_fixes.csv`, applied by `tools/gen_loot.py`, so the mod only uses exact
game names. The dev command `loot check` lists the reward names that match nothing in the game.

Known but not given yet:
- Crayburn Castle (dungeon not implemented): see the quest rewards above.
- Dungeon packs (Tranquil Dream, Fort Romor, The Usurper, Smoldering Dead, Devonshire Keep): their
  contents are in the loot tables, the dungeons are not implemented.
- AZ2.

Champion levels follow the game's XP table (Game): level 2 at 1,000 XP … 15 at 110,000.
Talent points per level: 1 (PLACEHOLDER).

## Tutorial

The game's own tutorial (5 scripted battles, Game). It starts after creating an account when "Play
the tutorial" is ticked, as the game did after account creation; unlike the game's first tutorial it
can be left from the menu. Between rounds the game shows its "Tutorial Rewards" window ("You've been
rewarded with some new cards!", Game) with 3 cards: these cards are given, once per account
(progression accounts; an "unlock everything" account already owns them).

## Account level (Wiki2019)

- Account XP per win: 50 for PvE (campaign, Quick Match and practice against the AI).
- The first 1,200 XP of each period count double; cap 4,000 XP per period (8,000 from level 20);
  the period resets 20 hours after its first win.
- XP per level: 1,000 (level 2) rising to 7,000 (level 41 and up); maximum level 100.
- Rewards by level: levels 2-40 as listed by the wiki, then the 10-level cycle the game used.
  The XP bar at the bottom left of the main menu shows level and daily XP; rewards are given
  automatically on level up (the game made you claim them).

## Packs

**Campaign packs** (Howling Plains = "Adventure Zone 1" pack; dungeon packs alike), Video (25+ packs
opened on screen): 2 commons of the first PvP sets (Shards of Fate, Shattered Destiny, Armies of Myth),
1 card of the pack's own list (every pack), 1 equipment, then a 2nd equipment or, about 1 time in 3, a
stardust (Common, Uncommon, Rare; Legendary seen once; weights PLACEHOLDER). Which card and equipment:
LootTables2016 (1,162 AZ1 packs), each picked weighted by its %; the unique equipment 1 time in 100.
The sheet's "cards 50%" only counted the zone cards and is not used as a per-pack chance any more.

**Boosters** (store, current set): 17 cards per pack (Forum2017: 3 packs = 51 cards). Rarity split
PLACEHOLDER: 1 top card (legendary 1 time in 8, otherwise epic 1 time in 5 in the sets that have
epics, otherwise rare), 3 uncommons, 13 commons, no basic shards.

**Alachian Sea packs** (AZ2): no data. The AZ2 set has no commons in the game data: the booster split
above is used with uncommons in the common slots (PLACEHOLDER).

## Matches outside the campaign

Quick Match and practice wins give account XP only: no source mentions gold or cards for AI
matches outside the campaign and the arena.
