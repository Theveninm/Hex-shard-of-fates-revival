# Progression, rewards and store

The original game decided rewards and store prices on its servers; the client files only hold
the champion XP table and the starter decks. HEX Revival therefore rebuilds progression from period
sources. This page lists every value with its source, so that **placeholders** (values chosen for
the mod, without a source) can be replaced when better information turns up.

All of it applies to accounts created without "Unlock everything" (those already own every card,
so card rewards are skipped; gold, XP and levels still count).

Code: `mods/HexRevival/src/HexRevival/Progression.cs` (rewards, levels), `Loot.cs` (campaign
packs, data generated by `tools/gen_loot.py`), `Store.cs`, `Collection.cs` (starter collection),
`Inventory.cs` (equipment earned). Each value is tagged there with `Src.*`.

### Dungeons

The AZ1 dungeons are played on their own map (the client's dungeon prefabs), entered from their AZ1
node and replayable once finished: Castle Crayburn (the starting dungeon of every new champion),
the Tranquil Dream (Node040), Fort Romor (Node011, Underworld quest), The Usurper (Node008, Ardent
quest), the Smoldering Dead (Node024) and Devonshire Keep (Node068). The game data only lists the
rooms; each room is rebuilt as conversation → battle → success / fail conversation from the
conversation and encounter names (Game) and the videos. Rules:

- Dungeon lives (Game: TalentManager.GetTotalDungeonLives, 3 + talents): a lost battle costs one, the
  room can be tried again; the last one ends the run (the game's "Dungeon failed" window, back to
  the AZ1 map, everything to do again). The lives left are shown by the mod (the client does not).
  Castle Crayburn has no limit. Leaving a dungeon (back button) abandons the run.
- Dialogue choices (Game: the answers' events): the Thorn Knight's crypt can be left alone, the
  Minstrel's Gallery tripwires disabled (no battle), the Oratory's battle is the burning zombies
  (ruby pried out, "Devonshire Keep H2") or the dead knight ("H1").
- The Tranquil Dream: each dream creature has its own room, the one named after it (Game: the dungeon
  template's items, Dream Turtle NodeB, Skarn NodeC, Spider NodeD, Bear NodeE, Eagle NodeF). The videos
  show different fight orders, which are the players' choices of room, not creatures moving between
  rooms (the mod drew them at random until 1.2.3); its strength follows the fight's rank (Video: 12, 12, 18, 18, 24 health, i.e. Easy,
  Easy, Medium, Medium, Hard, in runs with different orders and answers), as do the rewards.
  Sister Midnight's form ("an echo of your will", Game) comes from the total of the five tales'
  answers (Game: one/two/three_point_answer). Video (MyHotStream, answers read from the dialogue
  pointer): total 6 -> Conquest (Underworld 2016 and Ardent 2017 runs), total 11 -> Retribution
  (2018 run); JBStevenson (2018, Shin'hare): total 8 -> Savagery (its tooltip "Form of Savagery",
  power Ferocious Might); "What did you learn?" does not count. Cunning and Instinct never seen:
  12-13 Cunning, 14-15 Instinct, and the limits 7/8, 9/10 are PLACEHOLDER (three data points only).
- Fort Romor: rooms in the template's order, as the gate's description places them (Game);
  each room's foe is its conversation's speaker: Stables → Legionnaire of Gawaine, Barracks →
  Rhonwen, Command Post → Commander Hawthorne; the Defense (3 battles: Lords Benjamin, Alexander,
  Adam) opens once the three are taken.
- Taming battles ("Tame X and win match", the Tamed quest): won without taming the creature (the Taming
  Sphere's 2-mana ability only has a 50% chance, Game), the battle stays available to fight again.
- Taming objectives count in the battles whose encounter mods hold the "Taming X" card (Src.Game: EncounterScene
  m_Mods), not the encounter named by the quest template: "Tame Bubblebee" names Foul Fae (Node 32), the Bubblebee is
  tamed against the Water Elemental (Node 31). A creature already tamed by the champion (reward claim) counts.
- The Usurper: Verrev is fought in Last Rites, after its dialogue (Video: MyHotStream Ardent 2017-03-22). The
  game data's node events are shifted by one node in the Usurper, the Tranquil Dream and Fort Romor (each node
  fires the previous node's battle), so they are not used to place battles; the node titles, the conversation
  owners and the videos are. The Banshee (NodeG) has no conversation or encounter in the game data.
- The Usurper: Shepherd's Rest battle by who is dead (Ghuuhg in the Hall of Vermin, Mistsim in the
  Chamber of Awakening, or both); one of the two halls is enough to go on.
- Finishing a dungeon completes the quest objectives naming it (the Usurper quest's objective names
  Fort Romor's template, a game data error, handled).
- Not done: the first-attempt bonus gold of the game's hint ("a small amount of bonus gold if you
  manage to defeat an enemy on your first attempt": amount unknown); the Crayburn watchtower's
  training battle (its encounter is not in the game data).

## Campaign (AZ2, the Alachian Sea)

The AZ2 map (Game: its own DungeonTemplate and map prefab, shared background) is reached from AZ1 by the
skiff of "River of Whispers" and left by the "Transfer To AZ1" node. The faction hubs are Wakuna (Ardent)
and Naagaan (Underworld): only the champion's faction's hub has NPCs. The template's A/B node variants
(Ardent / Underworld, a demon swap at Harp Rock) are one map node each, the battle chosen by faction or
by the dialogue's event. Quest objectives without ids in the templates are matched by encounter name
(`Quests.AZ2Objectives`; the Mithral River and Raiders of the Crossed Dark fights are PLACEHOLDER).

### Sea voyages (Game)

- Portsmeare Harbor (Node019B): the ship is named once ("Seas The Day", "Kraken's Gold", "The
  Reckoning", "The Windy Way"), then the game's Ship Port window opens there: boat talents, the ship's
  deck (the "Edit Deck" button), "Set Sail".
- The ship is a sub-champion of class Boat (template "Boat", 40 health, Tattered Banner and cannon
  abilities), with its own deck (first a copy of the champion's deck) and talents. Ship talent points
  (Video): 3 when the ship is named, 1 for the first win over the Cloud Queen, the Cloud King, the Storm
  of the Century and Bloodhorn (7 in all, as on the videos' Ship Port); shown as a talent point line of
  the reward window (the original said "Ship Talent Point"). Sea battles' XP goes to the champion (Video).
- Hull (the ship's health): 40, or 60 with "Heavy Hull", kept from one sea battle to the next of a
  voyage (Game: Basic Hull "The Hull of your ship persists across encounters", the Ship Port "When
  running low on hull, you can return here for repairs"; the client reports the battle's end health, +5
  with Repair Crew, and reads a starting health back; Video: KVC Jan 2017). Full again at each new voyage
  and at Water Town ("The shipwrights of the town offer to repair the damage to your hull.", Game).
  MyHotStream (late 2017) shows full hull at every battle, which no talent or ability explains: not
  followed. A sea battle lost sinks the ship: the game's "Your boat has sunk!" message, back to the
  harbor (Video, same text).
- Coralcove Rock (once per champion, Video): the Mermaid's Blessing, "Your starting hand size is
  increased by 1" for the next battle: the game's "Mermaid's Blessing" effect card (8 cards, checked in game). The
  Lighthouse gives the card Sea Spirit (Video).
- Sea battles are fought again on each voyage. The three sea quests ("Pirates of the Alachian",
  "Stormwatch", "Lobster Tale": "Must defeat all 3 in one voyage") are given by the harbor master at
  the end of the ship naming, all at once, with the talent points (Video: KVC 002 1:28:09, MyHotStream
  2017-06-22 1:58; no quest window, only the tracker; the harbor master's name and portrait are never
  shown); their progress is
  reset when a voyage begins or ends; the harbor master (Node00B) takes the turn-ins ("Return to port"
  also ends the voyage). Rewards (Video): Pirates of the Alachian 500 gold, 1000 XP, Hired Horn Captain;
  Stormwatch 500 / 1000 + AZ2 pack; Lobster Tale 500 / 1000 + Hardshell Sealord; the three done: Alachian
  Sea Sleeve. A sea battle won again pays half its gold and a fifth of its XP (KVC: Overseer 200 / 130).
- Zodiac Docks (NodeB0X): disembark at the Mouth of the Zodiac River (Node033), the end of "The New
  Land".

### Mercenaries

A mercenary reward (Video: the reward window "MERCPAC" line and the game's hint "You have just earned a
mercenary and a party slot!") gives the mercenary, his starter deck's cards, his deck as a profile deck
template, and one party slot (account flag CAMP_PARTYCAP, capped by race by the client). The party, edited
with the game's own party screen, is saved per champion. Choosing a mercenary on the "Prepare for Battle"
panel makes him play the battle with his deck and his card as champion (the game's InstantiateMercenaryCard);
the battle's rewards go to the champion (Game: the client shows them to the champion). Mercenary leveling
(MercLeveling) is not reproduced: the game data has one upgraded version of each mercenary, and a 2016 Steam
thread gives two won "mercenary challenge" dungeon battles plus 25,000 gold per upgrade, but which battles were
challenges and their bonus gold are unknown.

## Siege

The original Siege (2018) was asynchronous PvP: a player stakes gold or platinum (the bounty) on a
defense of 3 of their decks, played by the AI with a personality chosen per deck; other players pay a
fee to attack it and must beat the 3 decks in a row, in one session. Everything was decided by the
server; the mod answers the client's Siege requests itself (`Siege.cs`).

| What | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum bounty | 1000 gold or 50 platinum | Video (rules screen, MyHotStream 2018-05-29 and 2018-06-03) |
| Fee to attack | 40% of the bounty, rounded up | Video (rules screen; every row of the keep list) |
| Fee split | 50% to the defender at once, 37.5% added to the bounty, the rest lost (all rounded up) | Video (rules screen; the defender's "-570" after two attacks on 1000) |
| Attack won (3 decks beaten) | the attacker takes the whole bounty and the Siege Sacks; the defender gets the Siege Sacks too | Video (A 31:49 "Earned: 1150 gold, 11 sacks"; A 0:40 defender "Lost: -570, 15 sacks") |
| Attack lost or withdrawn | the defense keeps its bounty (+1 win), no refund of the fee | Video (A 7:52) |
| Withdrawing a defense | the bounty comes back, no Siege Sacks | Video (rules screen) |
| Siege Sacks of a defense | 2 per hour of age + 1 per 1000 gold (or 1 per 5 platinum) of bounty, at most 500 | Game (the client's own formula) |
| Between two games | 27 s countdown, the game loads at 9 s | Video (A 1:50-2:17) |
| Rules of the battles | the game's own Siege rules (20-minute clocks, the defender's message, equipment allowed for the defender) | Game |

Without other players:
- **Defenses to attack**: the defenses of the Siege AI configuration (see AI opponents; PLACEHOLDER defaults:
  40 in gold, 10 in platinum, each 3 of the game's prebuilt decks with an AI style and an invented player name, most
  at the minimum bounty, some more: Video, the keep list shows mostly 1000-gold defenses, a few up to 93,056). Their
  record (`HexRevivalSaves/siege_world.json`) follows the bounty rule; a defense taken comes back with its starting
  bounty. The source of defenses is an interface (`IKeepSource`): other players' defenses could come from a server.
- **Attacks on the player's defense**: launched by the player from the defense screen, AI against AI (see AI
  opponents). The other defenses of the list are attacked by simulated players (PLACEHOLDER: one attack every
  2 hours on average, each defense deck beaten half of the time), so that the list lives.
- No Siege battle gives account XP (PLACEHOLDER: not known).
- Siege Sacks are spent in the Store's Prize Exchange tab (see Store). Not done: replays of the
  defense's battles (the log's replay buttons) are not recorded.

## AI opponents

The modes that had other players draw their opponents from an AI configuration, edited from "Configure the AIs"
at the bottom right of each mode's screen (Battlegrounds: the selected event's mode; Siege: the attack screen sets
the defenses to attack, the defense screen the attackers):

- **Ladder, constructed gauntlet, Frost Ring Arena**: tiers (ladder stars, gauntlet wins, arena tier), each listing
  decks: shared decks (made for players or for the AI), the game's own AI decks, or its encounters. Each deck of a
  tier has a frequency; in the arena, a role too: common, elite or boss (a tier's last fight is its boss, tiers 2 to
  4 have elite fights, Src.Video). Uruunaz and Zakiir, "extremely rare" (Src.Steam2016), are common decks with a low
  frequency (2% of the common fights where they can appear, PLACEHOLDER) and keep their own rewards.
- **Sealed and draft**: AI players (name, AI level, randomness, modifiers, frequency). Each builds its deck from its
  own cards (its 6 boosters, or what it drafted at the player's table: the 7 seats are drawn from these players);
  the decks of the runs in progress are shown and can be copied.
- **Siege, defenses to attack**: defenses set up like the game's defense screen (name, gold or platinum, starting
  bounty, 3 decks with their style, message), PLACEHOLDER defaults of 40 gold and 10 platinum defenses of the game's
  prebuilt decks. A defense taken comes back with its starting bounty.
- **Siege, attackers**: tiers by the defense's bounty listing decks (PLACEHOLDER default: the game's prebuilt
  decks). On the defense screen, "Launch an attack" makes one of them attack the player's defense: AI against AI,
  the player watching (a button speeds the game up x4 until pressed again or the game ends). As for a player's
  attack, the fee is paid (the defender's share to the player, the rest to the bounty) and the 3 defense decks must
  be beaten in turn; the defense decks play with the AI settings of the shared deck they come from (same name and
  champion), or the defaults.

What a deck's AI does is set on the deck (deck editor):

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| AI level | the game AI's own levels (EDifficulty, cumulative: each adds a skill). Only the 7 that change its play are offered: Easy, + fast cards, + combat tricks, Medium (+ builds its board), + bluffs with one resource, + bluffs when attacking, Hard (+ removes threats early); the game never checks AttackCorrectly, DoubleBlocking and BluffWithManyResources |
| Randomness of its choices | the AI lowers each card value it computes by a random 50 to 100% of this many hundredths (the game's default 50, 0 = none) |
| Style | the deck's personality: weighs the AI's choices (Aggressive, Burn...) |
| Modifiers (AI and player) | health, resources, charges, extra cards, cards in play, shards at the start: applied after the mulligan |

The level the original server gave the AI is not in the game's files (the client never sets it): every deck plays at
the AI code's own default, Hard, unless set otherwise (the campaign and its encounters too).

Decks outside the campaign are shared by every account (`HexRevivalSaves/decks_common.txt`, their AI settings in
`decks_common_ai.json`). The configuration of a mode is per account (`ai.json` in the account's folder, only the modes
it changed); the others use `HexRevivalSaves/ai_defaults.json`, which "Change the default settings" edits for every
account that kept them. To share opponents, copy these files.
